## v2 — Approvals Engine, Bonus Stock & HR

### Separate login areas
- `/admin/` → `AdminController` + `Auth::requireArea('admin', 'admin/login')`. Only users with role `owner` or `admin` reach any page under `admin/*` — the index.php router checks this for every request before dispatching.
- `/management/` → `ManagementController` + `Auth::requireArea('management', 'management/login')`. Owner/admin/manager/accountant allowed.
- Both areas share the session but authorize independently; the main ERP stays on `/index.php` for everyone.

### Approvals engine (`app/services/Approvals.php`)
- `Approvals::create()` — inserts a pending row into `approval_requests` with a unique constraint on `(reference_type, reference_id, request_type)` — duplicate submissions collapse to one request.
- `Approvals::decide(id, approve, note, deciderId)` — inside one transaction: validates request exists (pending), requester ≠ decider, writes decision, sets the source document's status, **and posts its journal** (via the existing posting service). Managers cannot approve.
- Outgoing documents exist in three states: `pending` (no balance effect) → `posted` (balances move, exactly once) OR `rejected` (balances untouched; decision retained).
- Dispatch targets: payments, bank_transfers, expenses, payroll, salary_advances, employee_loans, sales_invoices (below-minimum), salesman_issues (restricted bonus).
- Approvals cannot post twice — `journal_entry_id` existence + document `status` flip are enforced inside the same transaction that posts.

### Salesman stock & bonus
- `salesman_issues` gains `issue_type` (`transfer` vs `credit_issue`), `total_payable`, `total_bonus_cost`, `bonus_scheme`, `authorized_by`.
- `salesman_issue_items` gains `paid_qty`, `bonus_qty`, `issue_rate`, `line_payable`, `bonus_cost`.
- On approve of a restricted bonus issue: stock moves out of the source warehouse, journal posts Dr `SM_RECEIVABLE` / Cr `SALES` for the paid portion only, Dr `COGS` (paid at cost), Dr `PROMO` / Cr `INVENTORY` (bonus at cost). A `salesman_ledger` row advances the salesman's running balance.
- Unpaid free transfers move stock without any accounting (location change only).
- `salesman_ledger` — running balance per salesman: `+issue (debit)`, `-payment (credit)`, `-return (credit)`.

### Tax treatment of bonus lines
- Sales invoice bonus lines: stock moves out; revenue NOT posted (no `SALES` credit for the bonus line); cost posts Dr `PROMO`, Cr `INVENTORY`.
- Purchase invoice bonus lines: stock moves in at **unit_cost 0**; payable NOT increased; tax NOT charged on bonus lines.
- Customer discounts below `min_price` create a pending `sales_invoices` row (stock does not move) and an approval request — only after approve does the sale post.

### Payroll (extended)
- Salary types: `monthly`, `weekly` (rate × days ÷ 6), `daily` (rate × days), `hourly` (rate × hours), `contract` (fixed for the period).
- `payroll_deductions` table links advances and loans to payslips — enables exact reversal.
- Advances: create approval request → approve → disburse (Dr `EMP_ADV` / Cr `Cash/Bank`) → `deducted` tracked per advance; auto-deducted at next payroll (full or capped).
- Loans: separate ledger (`employee_loans` + `loan_installments`); weekly or monthly installment schedule; installments due ≤ period end deduct automatically.

### Salesman account — salary, advances, expenses, settlement (v7)
- `salesman_salary_payments`: approval-gated salary payments (id, salesman_id, date, amount, account_id, method cash|bank, period YYYY-MM, status, journal_entry_id). Owner/Admin post inline; others via Approvals engine (request_type `payroll`, reference_type `salesman_salary_payments`). `Posting::postSalesmanSalary` flips pending→paid in the same transaction with idem key `salesman_salary:<id>`.
- `salesman_salary_advances`: approval-gated salary advances; same flow; `posted` flips via `postSalesmanAdvance` with idem key `salesman_advance:<id>`.
- `salesman_expenses`: posted expense lines by type (diesel|vehicle|food|hotel|repair|other); inline post for Owner/Admin, Approvals otherwise (`salesman_expenses`); `postSalesmanExpense` books Dr `SM_EXP` (new chart account 5710 "Salesman Expenses"), Cr cash/bank.
- `Posting` handles all three exactly-once (status flip inside the transaction, journal idem keys).
- Account hub `salesmen/{id}/account`: 13 cards (goods issued/sold/stock, goods payable, bonus, diesel/food/other expenses, salary earned/paid/remaining, advances, final balance) + date-wise unified history.
- Salary accrual: months since `join_date` (inclusive of current month) × `salary` column; outstanding = accrued − paid; advances deduct automatically from future salary runs.
- Settlement page `salesmen/{id}/settlement`: every component (goods payable, collections, salary remaining, expenses, advances) plus net balance; positive = salesman owes company.
- Warehouses consolidated to **ONE main warehouse** (WH-MAIN, type `central`); factory warehouse deactivated (data preserved, stock merged to MAIN). Salesman vans keep their stock-location role but are hidden from FG pickers (type != 'salesman').
- `payroll.amount_paid` accumulates approved payments per payslip; status transitions `draft → partial → paid` (and back to `draft/partial` when a payment is rejected).
- New `payroll_payments` table holds each payment: `date`, `amount`, `account_id`, `notes`, `status` (pending|paid|rejected|reversed), `journal_entry_id`. Exactly-once via status flip inside the approval transaction + journal idem key `payroll_payment:<payment_id>`.
- Payment request flow: payroll page "Pay Balance / Submit for Approval" accepts an amount (blank = full remaining; smaller = partial) → row in `payroll_payments` (pending) → approval request with `request_type=payroll_payment`, `reference_type=payroll_payments` → Owner/Admin decision in Admin Console → posts (Dr Salaries & Wages / Cr cash-bank) or rejected. Multiple approved partials may share one payslip — the approvals unique key is per payment row, not per payslip.
- Over-payment refused at approval time: `Posting::postPayrollPayment` throws when the amount exceeds remaining; the approval stays pending (transaction rolls back).
- Reverses: `payrollReverse` reverses every approved `payroll_payments` journal for the payslip (plus the legacy single journal if any), zeroes `amount_paid`, releases deductions.
